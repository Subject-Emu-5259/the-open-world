## Full Description (App Directory/Steam)

### THE OPEN WORLD
*A Life Simulation Built for Reddit*

Welcome to the Mid-South. Your story begins in West Memphis, Arkansas — a small town with big dreams just across the river from Memphis, Tennessee.

**What makes THE OPEN WORLD different:**

🏙️ **Three Cities, Real Geography**
- Memphis, TN — Blues, BBQ, and big-city hustle
- Little Rock, AR — Government town with steady opportunities
- Southaven, MS — Suburban life, family-friendly

👥 **42 Living NPCs**
- Each with schedules, personalities, and secrets
- Build relationships that unlock opportunities
- NPCs move through the world based on time of day

🌤️ **Dynamic Weather System**
- Weather affects work earnings (-15% in storms)
- Exploration costs more energy in extreme conditions
- NPC presence changes with the weather

🏠 **Property & Investment**
- Buy real estate with mortgage financing
- Collect rental income monthly
- Invest in stocks, bonds, crypto, or business partnerships

🚗 **Vehicle Ownership**
- Purchase sedans, SUVs, trucks, motorcycles
- Build your garage (up to 3 vehicles)
- Sell for 60% resale value

💼 **Career Progression**
- Start entry-level, work your way up
- 15+ job listings across industries
- Professional reputation system

## Quick Start Guide

1. Type **"apply"** to see job listings
2. Type **"apply warehouse"** to get hired
3. Type **"work"** to earn money
4. Type **"status"** to check your progress
5. Type **"real-estate"** to see properties
