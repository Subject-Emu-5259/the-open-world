Welcome to **THE OPEN WORLD**.

This is the official subreddit for the game. THE OPEN WORLD is a Reddit-based city-life game where you build a character, take jobs, earn money, own property, make friends (and enemies), and shape your story across a growing map of cities.

### How to start playing

1. Join r/THEOPENWORLD
2. Look for the latest game post or pinned launch thread
3. Use game commands like `profile`, `explore`, `work`, `talk`, `shop`, and `move`
4. Check your progress and stats any time

### What you can do here

- Ask gameplay questions and get help
- Share screenshots, stories, and achievements
- Report bugs and suggest features
- Join community events
- Hang out with other players

### Quick rules

- Be respectful to players and mods
- No spam, self-promotion, or off-topic posts
- Bugs and exploits should be reported, not abused
- Keep feedback constructive
- Have fun

If you're new, drop a comment below and say hi.

— The Dev Team
