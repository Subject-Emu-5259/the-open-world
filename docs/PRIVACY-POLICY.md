# THE OPEN WORLD — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 25, 2026

THE OPEN WORLD stores player progress using Reddit Devvit's server-side Redis storage so saves can persist across sessions. We do not collect personal information beyond what Reddit makes available to Devvit apps for normal operation.

No data is sold, shared with advertisers, or used for profiling outside the game.

For questions or data-removal requests, contact the developer through the subreddit.
