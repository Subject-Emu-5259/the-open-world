# In-Game Help Reference THE OPEN WORLD — A Quick Start Guide

Welcome to **THE OPEN WORLD**, a city-life simulation game on Reddit.
You create a life, build a career, buy property, race cars, make friends, run faction ops, and deal with whatever random events the city throws at you.

## Getting Started

When you start the game, you'll choose a **background**. That background gives you starting money, skills, and sometimes a special perk.

From there, everything is driven by **text commands**. Type a command and the game tells you what happens next.

## Core Commands

| Command | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `status` | Your full life snapshot — money, job, skills, energy, time, weather |
| `help` | Full command list (also shows tips) |
| `sleep` | Rest and recover energy |
| `work` | Work a shift at your current job |
| `apply` | See job listings. Use `apply [job name]` to get hired |
| `travel [city]` | Move to another city |
| `explore` | Look around and find NPCs or events |
| `goto [district]` | Move to a different district |

## Money — Earn, Save, Spend

Most new players start with the `apply` command, get a job, and use `work` to build up cash.

- **Deposit/withdraw** money with the bank: `deposit 500` or `withdraw 500`
- **Study** to unlock better careers: `enroll city_college ai_software_eng`, then `study hours 4`
- **Gym** boosts fitness: `gym`

> Don't forget to `sleep`. Running out of energy hurts your pay and health.

## Buying Property

Once you have money, use `real-estate` to see listings in your current city.

### Examples

- `buy property Wicker Park Duplex`
  - If you have the full price, you buy it outright (cash).
- `buy property Orange Mound Duplex cash`
  - Force cash payment.
- `buy property Uptown Condo mortgage`
  - Put 20% down and finance the rest.

Mortgaged properties still earn rent, but you get a monthly mortgage bill. Owned properties build equity and can be sold later.

Check your portfolio with `properties`.

## Social & Factions

- `people` — see nearby NPCs
- `talk [name]` — chat
- `greet [name]` — build rapport
- `assist [name]` — help with a quest
- `factions` — see your groups
- `support [id]` / `sabotage [id]` — influence faction wars
- `chat [id]` — enter a group chat

NPCs remember your name, your relationship status, and recent conversations.

## Vehicles & Racing

- `buy vehicle sports` — add a car
- `maintain [name]` — keep it running
- `race [track]` — enter a single race
- `race season` — start the World Street Series championship
- `race standings` — check points

## Quests & Events

Use `quests` to see active missions. NPCs give quests when you talk or assist them.

Random city events can trigger when you explore. Use `event` and `event choice [id]` to respond.

## Five Tips for New Players

1. **Get a job first.** Use `apply` until hired, then `work` regularly.
2. **Bank your money.** Cash on hand can be lost to random events.
3. **Study early.** Education unlocks higher-paying careers.
4. **Sleep before you're drained.** Low energy tanks your income.
5. **Explore every few commands.** NPCs and events refresh as time passes.
